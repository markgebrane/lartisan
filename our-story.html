Our Story

THE MAN WHO NEVER RUSHED A POUR.

Before L'Artisan Alcoolique became a brand, there was simply a man, a workbench, and a glass of whiskey. He believed that a good whiskey deserved more than to be poured and forgotten. And he understood something simple: the best part of whiskey isn't always the whiskey. It's the moment around it. That idea became L'Artisan Alcoolique.

The name

To the craftsman,
the work is never rushed.

L'Artisan is the maker who sits with his craft long after the room has gone quiet — patient, precise, a glass within reach. We built this kit in that spirit: not a gadget, but an instrument. Every case engraved, every torch weighted, every flavour chosen — because the difference between a drink and a ritual is care.

— L'Artisan Alcoolique, Beirut
