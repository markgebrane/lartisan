Questions

What makes L'Artisan different from other whiskey smoker kits?
Every kit is hand-finished in Beirut with a solid engraved oak case and six real wood flavours — a ritual, not a mass-produced gadget.
What's included in the kit?
The engraved oak case, the smoker top and barrel, a gold butane torch, and six wood-chip flavours — everything to smoke your first pour out of the box.
Do I need bartending experience?
None at all. If you can light a candle, you can smoke a drink — about 30 seconds from start to first sip.
Can I use it with drinks other than whiskey?
Absolutely — it's beautiful on whiskey, but works just as well on cocktails, rum and mezcal, even coffee, cheese or a steak.
Is this a good gift?
One of our most-gifted pieces. It arrives in a presentation-ready engraved case — ideal for the whiskey lover who has everything.
What if I'm not satisfied?
Message us and we'll make it right — a repair, a replacement, or a return. We stand behind every kit.
